# pcbrap
PCB rapid prototyping solution. 3D printer speed brought to PCBs.


## Plan:
For etching, it will use a chemical etchant bath of dilute acetic acid and peroxide. The etchant solution will be regenerated through electroplating of a nickel electrode to remove dissolved copper.

To transfer the pattern, paper masks will be combined with photoresist.

Photoresist and soldermask are wet film and will be spin coated.

The two types of wet film are added with a pair of syringes and a pair of lead screws.
